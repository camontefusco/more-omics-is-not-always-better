# Novelty-search notes — first pass

Date: 2026-08-21

The initial search found direct overlap with the proposed project in three areas:

1. FORGE evaluates the relative contribution of gene expression, SNP, and CNV information and reports that gene expression dominates while additional mutation/CNV information can worsen error.
2. DrEval provides a broad evaluation framework with modality ablation, realistic application-aware splits, and cross-study testing across major pharmacogenomic screens.
3. MKDR addresses missing modalities through completion and knowledge distillation in a cervical-cancer-focused setting.

These findings rule out novelty claims based solely on multimodal prediction, modality ablation, missing modalities, or cross-dataset evaluation. The defensible gap may instead lie in a pre-specified, pharmacology-aware synthesis of incremental information value across mechanisms of action, using simple interpretable models, explicit conflict/redundancy measures, missingness stress tests, and calibration/uncertainty under dataset shift.

This is provisional. The five strongest overlap papers, complete bibliographic metadata, and the final novelty statement remain pending.
