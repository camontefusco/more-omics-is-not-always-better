# Cross-dataset missingness audit

The current pooled exploratory audit covers 15 drug-level evaluations at 25% random expression-feature masking, with cell-line-grouped fit/calibration/test partitions and nominal 90% residual intervals.

| Dataset | Drugs | Mean coverage | Range | At/above 90% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GDSC1 | 5 | 90.0% | 85.4–94.8% | 2/5 |
| GDSC2 | 5 | 94.6% | 89.0–100.0% | 4/5 |
| CTD² | 5 | 88.6% | 82.7–91.9% | 2/5 |
| **Pooled** | **15** | **91.1%** | — | **8/15** |

The evidence supports a cautious conclusion: masked calibration materially improves uncertainty validity relative to the original unmasked calibration bug, but nominal coverage is not universal across drugs or datasets. The final paper should report drug- and dataset-stratified calibration, include repeated seeds, and avoid claiming a guaranteed 90% interval without a larger prespecified conformal evaluation.

The machine-readable aggregate is generated by `scripts/aggregate_missingness_results.py` and retained locally as `data/processed/cross_dataset_missingness_summary.json`.
