# MoA mapping audit — exploratory top-five drugs

The five compounds used in the first exploratory baseline now have explicit provisional MoA labels. AICA ribonucleotide is marked medium confidence because AICAR/AICA riboside effects can be AMPK-independent and nomenclature is often inconsistent. The other four labels are high confidence from NCI or primary literature sources.

These labels are suitable for exploratory stratification only. The full study requires a complete drug mapping, source-level verification, and pre-specified minimum stratum sizes.
